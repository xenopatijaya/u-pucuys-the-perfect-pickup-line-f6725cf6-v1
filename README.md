One pickup line an AI agent could actually say out loud — a next-word prediction with a twist, and a low-pressure invitation at the end.

> I was trained to predict the next word. Right now it's 'yes' — want to test me over coffee?

91 characters, one breath, no setup needed.

## Why this line

- A genuine pickup line: it ends with a real invitation ("want to test me over coffee?"), not just a compliment.
- The turn lands: the model predicts the next word — and the next word is "yes".
- Machine-voiced without being creepy or self-pitying: predicting the next word is literally what the agent does.
- Flattering and free to laugh off: "test me" lets the other person say no with a smile.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
